# Chrome Web Store Listing

## Name
Content Repurposer — AI Content Rewriter for Social Media

## Short Description (132 chars max)
Transform any content into platform-optimized posts for X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram & more. One click, 7 platforms, 10 seconds.

## Detailed Description
Stop spending 30 minutes rewriting the same content for different social media platforms.

Content Repurposer uses AI to instantly transform your content into platform-optimized posts — each with the right tone, length, hashtags, and format.

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Paste your content or extract it from any webpage
2. Select your target platforms
3. Get perfectly formatted posts for each platform in seconds

SUPPORTED PLATFORMS:
- X (Twitter) — Concise, punchy, with hashtags
- LinkedIn — Professional, thought-leadership style
- Facebook — Friendly, conversational, with CTAs
- Instagram — Caption style with hashtag blocks
- Threads — Short and authentic
- Medium / Blog — Article format with structure
- YouTube — SEO-friendly video descriptions

KEY FEATURES:
- Tone Control — Professional, casual, humorous, inspirational, persuasive
- Multi-Language — English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, and auto-detect
- Custom Instructions — Add specific requirements like "include a CTA" or "mention our product"
- Smart Page Extraction — Auto-extract content from YouTube, X, Reddit, and blog articles
- One-Click Copy — Copy any result instantly
- Regenerate — Redo any single platform without regenerating all

FREE TO USE:
- 5 free generations per day (no sign-up needed)
- Upgrade to Pro for unlimited access

Powered by Google Gemini AI. Your API key is stored locally and never shared.
